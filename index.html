Our craft

Three steps, never rushed

Every visit follows the same unhurried ritual — it's why Sale keeps coming back to Cross Street.

I

Consultation

We talk first. Face shape, hair type, how you style it at home — the cut is planned before a single strand falls.

II

Precision cut

Scissor-led cutting with measured clipper work, checked from every angle in the mirror before we call it done.

III

Finishing touches

Neckline razored, edges lined, a hot-lather finish on the neck — then the final look-over together, twice.

Tools of the trade

Steel stays sharp here. Scissors honed, razors stropped and towels steamed fresh — the old tools, kept to a new standard. It's a slower way to work, and you'll feel the difference the moment you settle into the chair.
