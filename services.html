The menu

Services, the old way

Four services and nothing else — we'd rather do four things beautifully than twenty things averagely.

Service 01

Precision Haircuts

The bread and butter. Every cut begins with a proper consultation — face shape, growth patterns, how you actually style your hair on a Monday morning — then scissor-led cutting with measured clipper work.

  • Consultation before every first visit
  • Scissor-led shaping, clipper detailing
  • Razored neckline and styled finish
Service 02

Beard Trims & Shaping

Beards are architecture. We map the lines to your jaw, carve them in with a steady hand, and finish with hot-lather edging so the shape holds its own between visits.

  • Line-up and full reshaping
  • Hot-lather edging for crisp lines
  • Conditioning oils to finish
Service 03

Hot-Towel Shaves

The full ritual, exactly as it should be: steamed towels to soften the skin, rich hand-whipped lather, and a straight razor handled with unhurried confidence. You'll leave wondering why you ever rushed this.

  • Steamed towels and rich lather
  • Straight-razor shave, unhurried
  • Cool towel and balm to close
Service 04

Junior Cuts

First cuts are milestones, not chores. We take our time, keep it good-humoured, and make sure your young gentleman walks out proud of the mirror.

  • Patient, friendly first cuts
  • Styles that suit school and play
  • Parents welcome in the chair-side seat
